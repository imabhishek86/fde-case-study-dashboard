# MoveInSync Engineering Backlog & Technical Investigation Plans

## Overview
This engineering backlog outlines the technical investigations, telemetry requirements, and architectural validation tasks necessary to support the product initiatives. To preserve architectural integrity, all technical root causes are treated as hypotheses until internal server, client, and fleet telemetry confirms them.

## Architectural Validation Principles
- **No Premature Technology Swaps:** Do not replace WebSockets, databases, or messaging brokers without conclusive telemetry proving they are the bottleneck.
- **Defensive Client Engineering:** All network-dependent features (OTP, ride details, driver contact) must maintain graceful offline and stale-state fallbacks.
